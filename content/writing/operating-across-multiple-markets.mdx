People ask why my work spans business strategy, trading cards, equities, and real estate. The simple answer is that all four domains reward the same skills: pattern recognition, risk framing, and disciplined execution.

I run one operating cadence for everything. Every week I review assumptions, track downside, and prioritize opportunities where conviction is high and complexity is manageable.

The most useful shift is treating every project like an allocation decision. Time, attention, and trust are the scarcest assets, so each commitment has to earn its place.

This site is built around that idea: one stream of work, multiple lenses, and transparent reasoning.
