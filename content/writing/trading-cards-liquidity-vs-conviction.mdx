Card collecting is emotional, and that is part of the fun. But if you are allocating real capital, you need a framework that separates taste from risk.

I split cards into three buckets: core long-term holds, tactical flips, and educational buys where the goal is to learn the market.

When liquidity dries up, I reduce tactical inventory first. I protect optionality so I can move when mispriced opportunities appear.

Conviction matters, but only if it survives honest stress testing.
