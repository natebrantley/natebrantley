The headline market has cooled, but neighborhood-level inventory is moving in very different ways. Broad averages hide the opportunities.

First, quality listings are still clearing quickly when priced with discipline. Second, financing creativity is becoming a bigger edge than pure offer price. Third, local credibility still wins negotiations.

For sellers, preparation and pricing are non-negotiable. For buyers, patience and clarity on must-haves matter more than trying to perfectly time macro headlines.

I continue to advise clients to focus on durable fit and five-year resilience rather than short-term noise.
