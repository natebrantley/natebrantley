Real estate and equities feel different day to day, but both require clear assumptions and scenario planning.

The same questions apply in both: what is mispriced, what can break the thesis, and what is the cost of being early?

I have found that cross-training across asset classes improves judgment because it reduces narrative bias.

The edge is rarely information alone. It is interpretation plus discipline.
