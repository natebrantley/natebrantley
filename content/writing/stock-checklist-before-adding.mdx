I try to avoid making equity decisions from momentum alone. A short checklist keeps me grounded when sentiment runs hot.

The seven checks are: business quality, management alignment, balance-sheet durability, valuation range, downside map, catalyst clarity, and thesis fragility.

If I cannot explain the bear case clearly, I am not done. If I cannot explain why now, I wait.

This process does not remove risk, but it keeps me from confusing confidence with discipline.
