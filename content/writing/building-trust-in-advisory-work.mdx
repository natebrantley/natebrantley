In advisory businesses, technical skill gets you in the room and trust keeps you there.

Most client frustration comes from ambiguity, not incompetence. Fast updates, explicit tradeoffs, and documented next steps solve more problems than flashy strategy decks.

I treat every engagement like a long-term partnership, even if the project window is short.

Clear communication compounds.
